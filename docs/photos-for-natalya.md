This is a private page for sending photos, videos, and voice recordings to Dave. Nothing is posted on the web. Only the two of you can use this page.

There are two galleries:

- **From me** — what you sent to Dave
- **From them** — what Dave sent to you

You can both open both galleries. Tap the one you want.

**Chat** is at the top of the page. It is one text conversation. Your messages are on the right. Tap **Photos** to go back to the galleries. Pictures stay in the galleries, with their captions.

## Sign in

1. Open the link in Safari on your iPhone, or in Chrome on a computer.
2. A Cloudflare page appears and asks you to sign in. Cloudflare is the lock in front of the page. It checks that you are you before the photos page opens.
3. Choose **Sign in with Google**.
4. If Google shows a list of accounts, pick **[natalyashapran1@gmail.com](mailto:natalyashapran1@gmail.com)**. Do not pick another account.
5. If Google asks you to continue or allow access, accept it.
6. You land on a page titled **Private Photos**.

The first time takes a few extra taps. After that, the phone often remembers you until the sign-in expires. If it asks again later, use the same Google account.

## Send something

1. Tap **Choose files**.
2. On an iPhone, pick photos or videos from the Photos app. For a voice recording, choose the voice memo (it is usually an `.m4a` file).
3. The page shows a preview. Tap the **×** on a preview if you want to leave that one out.
4. Tap **Send**. The button shows how many files are about to go, for example **Send 3 files**.
5. Wait until it says they were sent. Open **From me** to see them. Dave sees those same files under **From them**.

On a computer you can also drag files onto **Or drop files here**.

A few limits:

- Send up to 8 files at a time. If you have more, send them in another batch.
- Each photo or voice recording needs to be under 40 MB.
- Each video needs to be under 250 MB.
- iPhone photos (including HEIC), videos, and voice memos are fine. You do not need to convert them.
- Each file has a Caption box before you send. That text shows under the picture in the gallery and on the large view. You can change it later by opening the file and tapping Save.

## If you want to take one back

Open **From me**, tap the file, then tap **Delete**. It disappears from the gallery for both of you. Dave can put it back for 30 days. After that it is gone.

- A file fails to send: it may be over the size limit, or the batch may be more than 8. Send that one on its own.
